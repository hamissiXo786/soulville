About Soulville

A home for the spoken, the sung and the seen.

Soulville is a multidisciplinary creative platform dedicated to poetry, lyricism, Neo Soul music, visual arts and community engagement.

We create meaningful opportunities for artists and audiences to connect through shared experiences, from the first nervous verse at an open mic to a full evening of performance.
